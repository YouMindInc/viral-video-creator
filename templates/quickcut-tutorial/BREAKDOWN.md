# Quick-Cut Tutorial — style breakdown

A **Quick-Cut Tutorial** is a talking-head "how to do X with AI" short. It is fast to make and easy to produce in volume.

**Spine:** one continuous selfie recording carries the voice and the story from start to finish.

**Cutaways:** every 1.5–4 seconds the picture cuts away, while the voice keeps running underneath. It cuts to:
- a screen recording;
- the AI result;
- a phone mock-up;
- a big headline.

**Captions:** word-by-word, 2–4 words at a time, with the second line in yellow.

**Ending:** always *COMMENT "KEYWORD"* ("comment the keyword and I'll DM you the prompt").

## Style spec

**Structure**
- **Hook:** the first 2 s are either a kinetic line on the face, or the face in an inset over the most impressive AI result.
- **Middle:** "Proof → how-to → proof". Show the result, show the exact clicks (screen or phone recording), show the result again.
- **Pacing:** a new picture every 1.5–4 s. Back-to-back face cuts alternate between a normal and a punched-in (~112%) framing, like jump cuts.
- **Ending:** a call to action. "COMMENT" in white sans, then the keyword in yellow condensed type between spaced quotes. A profile card slides down from the top.

**Captions**
- 2–4 words per caption, each word popping in on its spoken time (0.14 s scale/fade).
- *Kinetic* (on the face): two centred lines. Line 1 is white, about 72 px; line 2 is yellow `#FFE81A`, about 96 px. Both are Poppins ExtraBold with a hard drop shadow.
- *Italic* (over footage and recordings): Poppins Bold Italic, about 46 px, white with a soft shadow. A highlighted word turns yellow.

**Headlines and stats**
- A condensed display face (Anton) at 104 / 168 / 220 px. A white connector line ("for", "and") sits above coloured key words.
- The palette is yellow `#FFE81A`, green `#3BE03F`, red `#F23B45`, pink `#F7A6F0` and orange `#FF9F1C`.
- They sit on the face, or on blurred footage tinted purple, orange or blue.

**Layout (1080×1920)**

| Element | Geometry |
|---|---|
| Split | Media 0–786; a 6 px yellow divider; face 792–1920 |
| Phone | 652×1410 body at 214,205 |
| Card | 810×1410 at 135,255 |
| Face inset | 792×820 at 144,1190, 58 px radius, 4 px white border |
| Face bubble | 480 px circle at 300,190, 9 px white border |
| Profile card | 780 px wide at 150,70 |

**Audio:** the voice from the selfie recording, with very quiet music underneath.

## Mapping to `template.json`

Scenes are listed by time. Any time not covered by a scene shows the full-screen face, with alternating punch-ins.

| Scene `type` | Use it for | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| `face` | The talking head, explicitly | — |
| `split` | A recording on top and the face below | `media` |
| `broll` | A full-screen result or footage | `media` |
| `phone` | A phone screen recording | `media`; `background`: `blur` or `#hex` (salmon with white circles: `#F3A291`) |
| `card` | A vertical result in a rounded card | `media`; `background` |
| `inset` | The hook: footage with the face card at the bottom | `media` |
| `bubble` | Footage with a round face bubble | `media` |
| `title` | A big condensed headline or stat card | `lines` `[{text, color, size: s\|m\|l, at}]`; `over`: `face` or `blur` (+ `media`, `tint`) |
| `cta` | The "COMMENT 'KEYWORD'" ending | `keyword`; uses the top-level `profile` |

Every scene also takes these options:
- `start` and `end`, in seconds on the talking-head clip;
- `mediaStart`, to trim the media;
- `fit` (`cover` or `contain`);
- `transition` (`cut` or `flash`);
- `captionStyle` (`kinetic`, `italic` or `none`) and `captionY`, which override the defaults above.

**Captions** come from a word-level SRT; `pnpm transcribe:quickcut-tutorial <video>` makes one.
- Put `*word*` around a word in the SRT to highlight it, or list words in `highlight`.
- Put `|` in the SRT to force a new caption.

**What isn't modelled one-to-one:**
- multi-image stacks and carousels (use `card` or `title` over `blur` with a clip instead);
- motion-blur whip transitions (use `flash`);
- the two-tone overlapping hook caption (the kinetic style approximates it).
